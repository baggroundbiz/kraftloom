Store Policies

Shipping & Delivery

We ship across India. Standard orders (in-stock items) are dispatched within 2-3 business days. Delivery within Bengaluru usually takes 1-2 days, while outstation orders may take 4-7 days depending on the pin code.

For express delivery within Bengaluru, please reach out to us on WhatsApp before placing your order to check local courier availability (Dunzo/Porter/Swiggy Genie).

Custom Orders

Custom orders are crafted specifically for you. Because of the handmade nature of these items, production timelines range from 5 to 15 days depending on complexity and current order volumes.

A 50% advance payment is required to confirm a custom order. Once work has begun, custom orders cannot be cancelled or modified.

Returns & Refunds

Due to the delicate and customized nature of our products, we do not accept returns or exchanges for change of mind.

If an item arrives damaged, please share an unboxing video with us on WhatsApp within 24 hours of delivery. We will review the issue and arrange a replacement or refund for genuine transit damages.
